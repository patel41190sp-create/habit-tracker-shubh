Privacy Policy

App: Habit Tracker Shubh  |  Package: com.shubh.habittracker  |  Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Habit Tracker Shubh (“the App”, “we”, “our”), developed by Shubh Patel, handles information when you use the App.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not run a server that collects your personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

2. Data stored on your device

Your habits, progress, settings and any PIN you set are stored only on your own device (in the App’s local storage). We cannot see this data. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information is deleted.

3. Reports and backups you create

When you export a PDF report or a backup file, the file is saved to the Downloads folder on your device. These files stay on your device unless you choose to share them yourself. On older Android versions (Android 9 and below) the App may ask for storage permission to save these files.

4. Advertising (Start.io)

The App shows ads provided by Start.io (a third-party advertising network). To show and measure ads, Start.io may collect and process certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, and information about how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Start.io under its own privacy policy, and we do not control it. Please review Start.io’s privacy policy on the Start.io website to learn more.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Permissions used

  • Internet / network state – to load ads and fonts used by the App.
  • Advertising ID – used by the ad network to show and measure ads.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) – to save PDF reports and backups to your Downloads folder.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data security

Because your habit data stays on your device, its security depends on your device’s own protection. We recommend keeping your device locked and keeping backups in a safe place.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the “Last updated” date changed above.
